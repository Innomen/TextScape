About TextScape

TextScape is a knowledge exploration tool that helps you visualize connections between ideas in your documents.

How It Works

Upload text files or paste text directly. TextScape breaks your content into semantic units and builds associations between related concepts using confabulation-inspired algorithms.

Exploring the Graph

Each node represents a fragment of text. Connected nodes share related concepts or ideas. Click on a node to see its content and explore related information.
